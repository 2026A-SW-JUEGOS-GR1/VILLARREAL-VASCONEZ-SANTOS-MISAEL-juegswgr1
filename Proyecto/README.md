## You are LeBron James.
Maybe not in real life, but in this game for sure.

## Features

**Collectibles:** You can collect basketballs, which you can then use for hooping by collecting hoop collectibles. You can also collect stars for no reason. The stars are bound by the tiles.

**Opponent:** Your mission besides hooping is to avoid Michael Jordan. He manifests himself in two ways: the bigger, slower version takes all your basketballs. Watch out, because if the small fast Michael Jordan gets you, you are out of the game!

**Different sprites:** When turning left or right, different sprites are shown.

**Infinite vertical climbing** You can play this all day and not reach the end.

**Cursor movements:** Use the *<* and *>* keys for horizontal movement. You can jump with *^* and go backwards with *down* key.

**Pink tiles:** These are all over the screen.

**Bounds:** Upon leaving the top or bottom of the screen, the game restarts. You can still be out of frame in the left or right.

**Event descriptions:** You get notified whenever you collect or collide.
